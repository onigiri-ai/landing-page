Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short

  • Onigiri Studio runs on your computer. Your games, plans, pictures and keys stay there.
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name or email to use the studio.
  • Usage and error reports are your choice. The studio asks the first time you open it, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings › Reports.
  • Reports never include your keys, what you type, what the AI writes back or your games. We delete them after 30 days.
  • Your AI requests go straight from your computer to your own AI provider. They never pass through us.

This summary helps you read the policy. It does not replace it.

1. Who we are

Onigiri Studio and the onigiri.ai website are made by Lantern Labs Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore (UEN 202117064E). In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Lantern Labs. We decide how the information described here is used.

This policy covers the Onigiri Studio desktop app (the "studio") and this website. It does not cover your AI provider or other services you connect, which have their own policies.

2. What stays on your computer

The studio is a desktop app, not an online service. It keeps the following on your computer, and none of it is sent to us:

  • your games, plans, pictures, code and their history
  • your settings
  • the keys you connect, protected by your system's own secure storage where your system has one
  • the studio's own log of errors, which it deletes after 14 days

You can delete any of it by deleting your game folders or uninstalling the studio and its data.

3. Usage and error reports (your choice)

The first time you open the studio, its welcome screen asks whether you want to share usage and error reports with us, with the switch in plain view. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Reports. When the switch is off, the studio sends no reports, stops recording at once and drops anything waiting to be sent.

We use reports only to find and fix problems and to decide what to improve next.

What a report can include

How you use the studioWhich screens and buttons you use, as fixed names, choices and counts. For example, that a game was started, a section opened or a picture drawn.
ErrorsWhat went wrong in the studio and where in its own code, with a short trail of the steps before it. Keys, email addresses and folder paths under your home folder are removed before anything leaves your computer.
A recording of the studio windowHow the studio's own screens change as you use them, so we can see where people get stuck. It is not a video of your screen and it records nothing outside the studio. Everything you type is hidden and your own words are replaced with stars. The game preview, key fields and your reference pictures are left out as blank boxes.
About the studioThe studio's version, your system's name and version (macOS, Windows or Linux) and the type of processor.
IdentifiersA random install number made by the studio, and a random number for each time you open it. They are not linked to your name, email, computer name or device.

What a report never includes

  • your keys, passwords or any other secret
  • what you type into the studio, your plans or your rules
  • what you ask the AI or what it writes back
  • your games, their files, pictures, code or name
  • your name, email address or the names of your folders

Who receives reports and for how long

Reports go to two companies that process them for us under contract:

  • PostHog, for usage and the recording, on its EU service in the European Union
  • Sentry, for errors, on its service in Germany

Like any connection over the internet, these services see your internet address when a report arrives. The studio asks PostHog not to use it to work out where you are, and we do not use it to identify you.

We keep reports for 30 days, after which they are deleted.

4. Services the studio connects to for you

Your AI provider

When you connect an AI provider, such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Cloud (Vertex) or Amazon Bedrock, the studio sends your requests from your computer straight to that provider with your own key. This includes what you ask for, parts of your game's plan and code and any pictures you add. These requests never pass through us and we cannot see them. Your provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to them.

Downloading tools

To write code and build games, the studio downloads tools from the companies that publish them:

  • Claude Code from Anthropic
  • Codex from OpenAI
  • Gemini CLI from Google, through the npm registry
  • git and some build tools from GitHub
  • the packages each game needs from the npm registry

Like any download, these services see your internet address. The studio runs Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI with their makers' own usage reporting switched off, as far as each tool allows.

Play on my phone

Play on my phone runs a small server on your own network, so a phone on the same Wi-Fi can open your game. It does not send your game anywhere outside that network.

5. This website

This website has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies of its own.

  • It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard records of visits, such as internet addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your internet address when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The studio's downloads are hosted on GitHub, which applies its own privacy statement.

If you write to us, we use your email address and your message only to reply and to keep a record of what we talked about.

6. Why we may use this information

We use reports from the studio only because you chose to share them, and you can withdraw that choice at any time in Settings › Reports. When you write to us, we use your message to answer you. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Where information is kept

We are based in Singapore. Reports are stored in the European Union by PostHog and Sentry, which may reach them from other countries to run their services under their own safeguards. Wherever information goes, we expect it to be protected to the standard this policy describes and the law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it and to take back your consent. To use any of these rights, write to privacy@onigiri.ai.

Because reports carry no name or email, we may need your help to find the ones that came from your studio. In any case they are deleted after 30 days. If you are not happy with our reply, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In Singapore this is the Personal Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

The studio and this website are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information about them. Your AI provider may have its own age rules.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters, for example a new kind of report, we will say so on the website and in the studio's release notes before it takes effect.

11. Contact us

For anything about your privacy, write to our data protection officer at privacy@onigiri.ai.
